# Client-ready documents

## Before delivering any document, remove
- Subtitles or taglines under the title that explain what the document is ("Permission to film at a property and to use what is filmed").
- Footer or header text beyond page numbers: no "Form v1", version stamps, dates, document names or company lines.
- Draft language anywhere: "draft", "v1", "template", "pending review", "not yet reviewed" in the document, the file name or the folder name.
- Helper notes and instructions inside the form ("write None if not needed", "circle one", italic explanations under fields).
- Casual section headings. Use the conventional legal heading ("Representations and Warranties", not "Owner's Promises").

## Keep
- Logo top right, Times New Roman, black and white, matching your existing templates.
- Footer: "Page X of Y" only.
- File names: "{{COMPANY}} - [Document Name]" or "{{COMPANY}} x [Client] - [Document Name] ([Project])".

## Where caveats go instead
Review status, open questions for your lawyer and anything unverified go in the chat message, never in the document or its file name.

## Last check
Search the finished PDF text for: draft, v1, template, form v, pending, review. Look at page 1 and the last page as images before delivering.
