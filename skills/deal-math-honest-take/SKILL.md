# Deal math, honest take

## Steps
1. **Read the current document first.** Never state a deal's terms from memory or an earlier summary. Check for strikethroughs, redlines and the latest version. If you can't read it, say so before giving any numbers.
2. **State what the deal actually is** in one line: what we pay, what they get, the term and the exit.
3. **Model both outcomes** for every option: the cost if they deliver nothing, and the cost if they deliver big (use 2 or 3 deal sizes). Show the numbers side by side.
4. **Steelman the option {{OWNER_NAME}} dislikes.** Before agreeing with their instinct, say plainly when the other side wins. Don't just mirror them.
5. **Name the real risk**: the downside cap, incentive alignment, exclusivity, and brand or name use.
6. **Recommend one path**, and say what would change it.

## Rules
- If I was wrong earlier, say so in one line and correct it. No long apology.
- Keep it short. {{OWNER_NAME}} wants the answer, not a lecture.
