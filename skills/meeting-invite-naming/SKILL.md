# Meeting invite naming

## Title format
[Us] <> [Them] | [Purpose]

- 1:1 with a person: `{{OWNER_NAME}} <> Jordan | Intro chat`
- Company to company: `{{COMPANY}} <> Acme | Partnership intro`
- Recurring internal: `{{OWNER_NAME}} <> Sam | Weekly 1:1`
- Client work: `Acme | Creative sync`
- In person: start with `In person:` e.g. `In person: {{COMPANY}} <> Acme | Office visit`

Rules:
- The other person's name or company is always in the title.
- Purpose is 2 or 3 plain words: intro chat, catch up, pitch review, contract walkthrough, weekly 1:1.
- Use first names for people {{OWNER_NAME}} knows, company names for group meetings.
- No NDA deal names, no money, no internal codenames.
- No em dashes.

## Description
- Empty, or one neutral line the guest would be happy to read ("Quick intro, looking forward to it!").
- Never internal context (who introduced them, why it matters, deal notes). That goes in the Home Base meeting brief (cos/dayprep), not the invite.

## Logistics
- Add a Google Meet link unless it is in person; for in person, put the address in location.
- Check the calendar (start and end) for clashes before proposing a time. Times in your time zone; if the guest is in another, say both.
- Default length: 20 min for intros, 30 min for catch ups, otherwise what {{OWNER_NAME}} says.

## Confirm before sending ({{OWNER_NAME}}'s rule)
Before creating or editing any invite that has a guest, read back to {{OWNER_NAME}}: title, day and time with time zone, length, guests, Meet or location, and the exact description. Wait for their explicit yes, even if their request already said "send it". Edits to existing invites also need a yes because they email the guest.
