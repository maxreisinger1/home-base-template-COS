# Hold proposed times

## When it fires
- A draft we write for {{OWNER_NAME}} that offers specific times (email card, Gmail draft, Slack, text).
- {{OWNER_NAME}}'s sent mail, Slack or texts that propose times (sweeps catch these).
- Someone else proposes a specific time and {{OWNER_NAME}} says yes, or it is urgent and {{OWNER_NAME}} hasn't answered yet.

## How to offer times
- Check the calendar first (start AND end of nearby events, travel time, all-hands, focus blocks).
- Urgent or important: offer ONE clear time (or two at most) with a deadline feel, not a menu.
- Routine: offer 2 to 3 options, loosely.
- Never offer a time that overlaps an existing event.

## Track pending offers (no calendar holds)
- Never create HOLD events on {{OWNER_NAME}}'s calendar.
- Keep a pending-offers list in project memory: who, slot(s), where offered (link), date offered.
- Remove an entry when the person picks a slot (the real invite replaces it), declines, or 5 business days pass with no reply (flag that in Home Base Waiting on).

## Conflict check
On every sweep, and whenever a new invite, offer or event appears, compare against the pending offers. Flag {{OWNER_NAME}} in the project chat when:
- A new invite or event lands on a slot {{OWNER_NAME}} has offered someone.
- {{OWNER_NAME}} (or a draft) offers the same slot to two different people.
- An offered slot is now blocked by something else on their calendar.
One short message: what conflicts, with whom, and a suggested fix. Otherwise stay quiet.

## Old holds
Delete any leftover HOLD: events from the old process, and tell {{OWNER_NAME}} in one line which were removed.
