# Reply on the right email chain

## Steps
1. Name the topic in a few words (e.g. "client SOW").
2. Search Gmail for the person AND topic words (SOW, contract, the document name, DocuSign), not just the person.
3. List the candidate chains with subject, date, and a one-line summary of what each is about.
4. Pick the chain where the document was sent or the topic was actually discussed. Most recent is a tiebreaker only.
5. If the document came only through DocuSign or another tool and no email chain discusses it, say so and ask {{OWNER_NAME}}: new email, or reply on a named chain.
6. Before saving, check the draft: subject matches the topic, recipients and cc match that chain, attachment present.
7. When you report the draft, name the chain (subject + date) and say in one line why you chose it.

Drafts only. Never send.
