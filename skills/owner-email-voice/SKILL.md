# {{OWNER_NAME}}'s email voice

This profile is built during onboarding from your last 500 or 1,000 sent emails (onboard-home-base, step 3). To rebuild it later, say "retrain my voice on my last 1,000 sent emails." The learn-owner-voice skill keeps it current after that.

## Defaults until the profile exists
- {{EMAIL_STYLE}}
- Short. One idea per paragraph. No lists or dashes in emails.
- Signed "{{OWNER_NAME}}".
- Reply on the chain about the topic (reply-on-right-email-chain).
- Drafts only. Never send.
