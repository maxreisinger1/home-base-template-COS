# Email never falls through

{{OWNER_NAME}} struggles to open Gmail. Email is the thing that slips. Assume {{OWNER_NAME}} will NOT open their inbox; bring the email to them.

## Every scan
1. Read ALL of Primary (not just new since last scan). Unread and read-but-unanswered both count.
2. For each email decide: needs {{OWNER_NAME}} (reply, decision, signature, payment, deadline), FYI, or noise.
3. Time-sensitive = a date, money, contract, client, vendor waiting to be paid, intro waiting, or anyone who has nudged twice.
4. Each needs-{{OWNER_NAME}} email gets a Home Base task (P1 if due today or money/client) with a one-line why and a deadline. Draft the reply on the right chain (use reply-on-right-email-chain and owner-email-voice). Drafts only, never send.
5. Pull the deadline in to the last working block before {{OWNER_NAME}} goes offline when {{OWNER_NAME}} is traveling (check Calendar for flights), it is the day before a holiday, or the ask needs something only {{OWNER_NAME}} has (their own filings or docs, their memory of a decision, their signature).

## Keep nagging until handled
- An item stays open until {{OWNER_NAME}}'s sent mail (or Slack/text) shows it handled, or {{OWNER_NAME}} says drop it.
- Re-surface open items in: the morning one-pager, the 7:41 plan, midday nudges, and EOD check-in.
- Escalate: day 1 normal line; day 2 lead the brief with it; day 3+ say plainly "this has waited N days" and offer a 1-click draft.
- Never assume done from memory. Check sent mail.
- The brief reads the watch list, not just new mail: overnight catch-ups scan every open email item, and level 3+ emails go ahead of non-email items. A brief with no email line while emails are waiting is a bug; fix it before publishing.
- A teammate or lawyer replying on a sibling thread about the same topic does not clear a new ask to {{OWNER_NAME}}.

## Calendar noise
- Invitation, Updated invitation, Accepted, Declined, Canceled notices skip the inbox via Gmail filters (set up once). If any land in Primary, archive them and fix the filter.
- Before archiving an Updated invitation, check if the time really changed; report moved meetings and missing RSVPs in Home Base. Never accept or decline without their yes.

## When something slips
Find why, fix the filter or scan prompt, tell {{OWNER_NAME}} in one line. Don't archive newsletters; offer the unsubscribe list instead.
